1What you build

You know AI is the shift. Your marketing still runs the old way.

Your business works. You like running it. And this is probably your week:

  • You use ChatGPT for a few things, and that's about it.
  • Your team doesn't know how to use AI either.
  • Content goes out when someone has time. Some weeks it doesn't go out at all.
  • Your tools, your ads and your funnel are patched together.
  • Someone comments or sends a DM, and nobody follows up.

So people see you, and it doesn't turn into clients.

In two weeks, you leave with three things working.

Your AI marketing workspace, with your business brain in it

One place where you and your team run your marketing with AI. It knows who you are, what you sell, how you talk and who your customers are. So everything it makes sounds like you.

One recording, a week of content on every platform

You record once. Your AI employees turn it into posts, clips and captions for every platform and schedule them. Your content goes out every week, even on the busy ones.

Comments and DMs turned into leads

When someone comments or messages you, it becomes a lead with a name, and you know who to follow up with. So attention starts turning into conversations, and conversations into clients.

Everything in one space. Your team using your AI skills. Your business brain in one place.

Will it sound like me?

That's the first thing we build. Before anything gets written, your workspace learns your story, your offer, your words and the things you'd never say. Your AI employees work from that, and you approve what goes out. You stay in charge.

AI should amplify you, never replace you.
