What "agentified" means

Agentified means your work is rebuilt so AI employees do the defined jobs, next to you.

"The restructuring of workflows, software, or entire organizations, so that autonomous, goal-oriented AI agents handle defined tasks alongside, or on behalf of a human."Enrique Marq

You decide how your marketing should run. Your AI employees take the jobs that repeat every week: cutting the video, writing for each platform, scheduling, sorting the DMs. You and your team keep the decisions, the taste and the people.

How the three days run.

  • Before we start: a short setup list and one video of 10 to 20 minutes from you. About an hour.
  • Each day: I show the step on my own workspace, you build it on yours while I go around the room.
  • Your round's private room: every recording, and one place to ask me questions.
  • Thirty days later: one more call to look at what your workspace made and what to build next.

After day 3 you leave knowing how to run your marketing with your AI employees, and how to add new ones.

Where this is going

Today your workspace runs on your computer, and you and one team member learn to work in it. Next, it moves online with accounts for your whole team, so everyone works and chats in the same place, next to the AI employees they build. Founding members get it first.

Who it's for

For founders with a business that works, and their teams.

Coaches, educators, info businesses and marketing service businesses, usually somewhere between $10K and $500K a month. What matters more than the type of business: you show up as yourself, you build your own thing, and you're optimistic about technology.

Not for You're starting from zero, or you want AI to run everything with nobody checking.
