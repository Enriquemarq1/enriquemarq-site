Why I built Nave, my AI content team

For the past two years, I struggled to let people know I existed. I was building my business alone in my little cubicle at home, and getting clients, getting anyone interested in what I had, felt impossible. I knew content was the way, but making it alone ate my whole week.

Do you have something great to offer to someone else, but it feels like nobody knows you exist?

Someone out there needs exactly what you have, and they’ll never get it if you can’t show up and say it.

So I built Nave: an AI content team that helps busy founders and creators show up authentically and create influence on social media.

The premise: a team trained for content production takes over the repetitive work, without the overhead of hiring, so it can empower and amplify one extremely capable person.
