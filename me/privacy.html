Your email & the free library

When you request the free library, Enrique Marq stores your email, signup source and signup time in his community’s Supabase project. The resource link appears after the save is confirmed.

If you want updates too

The optional checkbox records your request for future emails about new videos, essays and builds, including wins, fails and numbers. Leaving it unchecked still gives you the free library. Your email is not added to an active mailing campaign by this form. Email delivery is being set up separately; no confirmation or welcome email is sent by this form.

Before regular emails begin, the mailing service must provide address confirmation and an unsubscribe option. Existing resource-only signups are not enrolled automatically. Requesting resources or updates does not make you a paid community member.

Your choices

Your email is stored privately. To withdraw your request or ask for removal, contact @enriquemarqai on Instagram. We will add the business mailbox once it is ready.

The library is public on GitHub. Community and social links open other services with their own privacy policies. Zernio is an affiliate link. This hub includes no advertising trackers.
